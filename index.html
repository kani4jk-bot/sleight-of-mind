Meet the
Magicians

Two seniors at Crystal Springs Uplands School with a shared obsession for fooling their friends.

CAMERON

Cameron is a senior at Crystal Springs Uplands School. In his free time he enjoys reading, exercising, and hanging out with friends. Cameron began learning magic as a fun trick for his friends and has since developed his passion into the full-fledged magical thing it is today.

ETHAN

Ethan is a senior at Crystal Springs Uplands School. In his free time he enjoys running, playing with his dog, and eating with friends. Ethan began learning magic to keep him busy during school breaks, but he quickly learned his skill for visual deception is rarely found. Since then, Ethan has focused on mastering sleight of hand maneuvers to perform on friends.

FAQ

Quick answers to the things parents and event planners usually ask.

Do you really perform for free? +
Yes — we love doing this and we're not in it for the money. There's no charge for our shows.
How much space do you need? +
Not much! A small clear area where the audience can see us is plenty. Living rooms, classrooms, backyards — all great. No stage required.
Do you need any equipment from us? +
Just a small table or flat surface and seating for your guests. We bring everything else. No microphone or sound system needed for most rooms.
How far in advance should we book? +
A couple of weeks is usually safe, but reach out anytime — we'll do our best to make it work even on short notice.
Where do you perform? +
Anywhere in the Bay Area. Birthday parties, school events, community gatherings, fundraisers — if there's an audience, we'll come.
Is the show appropriate for kids? +
Absolutely. The show is built for all ages — kids love being volunteers, and parents tend to be just as fooled as they are.
